Known gaps against the shipped build
  - Dynamic Weather System, Cartoon FX Remaster, FastSky, Pulsar Neutron Star and MonoLimboStudio are Asset
    Store packages: scripts are stubs / shaders are placeholders until imported (docs/third-party-imports.md).
  - Side-by-side screenshots of the shipped build need Screen Recording permission for the terminal (the
    app runs on this Mac through Xcode — tools in car точно финал/.claude/skills/run-inferno-drive — but
    `screencapture` returns "could not create image from display" here); its console log was compared
    instead: the same game log lines appear ([Supabase] Клиент инициализирован., [GARAGE] Camera configured…).
